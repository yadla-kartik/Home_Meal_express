# 🍱 Home Meal Express

> Fresh, homemade meals delivered straight to your train seat.

---

## 🚂 About the Project

**Home Meal Express** is a mobile-first web application that connects train passengers with local home chefs near railway stations. Passengers can pre-order fresh, hygienic, and affordable homemade meals during their journey — delivered directly to their seat before the train departs the station.

Unlike existing platforms like IRCTC eCatering, RailRestro, or Zoop — which partner only with restaurants and hotels — Home Meal Express bridges the gap by empowering **home chefs and housewives** near stations to earn income by cooking and selling fresh food to travelers.

---

## ❌ The Problem

- Food available on railway platforms is mostly **unhygienic, overpriced, or low quality**
- Apps like Zomato/Swiggy are **expensive** and deliver hotel-made food, not homemade
- IRCTC eCatering and similar apps **only partner with restaurants**, not home chefs
- **No existing platform** connects passengers directly with local home cooks

---

## ✅ Our Solution

Home Meal Express provides:

- 🏠 **Homemade meals** from verified local home chefs near your station
- 🚉 **Seat delivery** — food reaches your coach and seat before the train leaves
- 💰 **Affordable pricing** — cheaper than restaurants, fresher than platform food
- 🧾 **PNR-based ordering** — enter PNR, we fetch your route and show nearby chefs
- ⭐ **Ratings & reviews** — verified passenger feedback on every chef and dish

---

## 👥 Modules

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| **User** | Passengers browse menus, place orders, track delivery, and rate meals |
| **Chef** | Home cooks register, upload menu, accept orders, and earn income |
| **Delivery Boy** | Platform-assigned delivery agents pick up and deliver food to train seats |
| **Admin** | Full control — approve chefs, manage orders, handle disputes, view analytics |
